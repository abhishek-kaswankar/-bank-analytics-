# 🏦 Bank Loan Performance & Risk Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This data analytics project evaluates the performance and risk associated with bank loans using datasets (`Finance_1.xlsx` & `Finance_2.xlsx`) of over 39,000 records[cite: 2]. The objective is to analyze customer behavior, repayment trends, and risk areas to drive smarter, data-driven banking decisions[cite: 3].

## 📊 Tools & Technologies Used
- **Microsoft Excel**: Data exploration, initial KPI implementation, and data formatting[cite: 2, 3].
- **MySQL**: Data querying, data cleaning, and joining multiple tables using advanced functions[cite: 2, 3].
- **Tableau**: Interactive data visualization and dynamic dashboarding[cite: 2, 3].
- **Power BI**: Advanced analytics, data modeling, and reporting[cite: 2, 3].

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Analyzed
1. **Year-wise Loan Amount Stats:** Tracked the continuous growth in loan demand from 2007 to 2011[cite: 2, 3].
2. **Grade and Sub-grade wise Revolving Balance:** Evaluated risk segmentation across different loan grades[cite: 2, 3].
3. **Total Payment for Verified vs. Non-Verified Status:** Compared repayment contributions based on customer verification[cite: 2, 3].
4. **State-wise and Month-wise Loan Status:** Monitored regional performance and monthly variations[cite: 2, 3].
5. **Home Ownership vs. Last Payment Date Stats:** Analyzed payment trends across different housing statuses (Mortgage, Rent, Own)[cite: 2, 3].

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- **Verification Impact:** Verified customers contribute to significantly higher payments (~58.88%), indicating a strong link between verification and repayment[cite: 3].
- **Risk Management:** Lower-grade borrowers present higher risks, highlighting the need for tighter approval processes and risk-based pricing[cite: 3].
- **Regional Targeting:** States like California (CA) and New York (NY) show the highest loan activity, suggesting areas for strategic expansion[cite: 3].
- **Ownership Trends:** Customers with existing mortgages contributed the highest to total payments, especially peaking around 2012–2013[cite: 3].

## ⚙️ Challenges Overcome
- Successfully handled missing values, duplicate records, and inconsistent date/number formats during the data cleaning phase[cite: 3].
- Built complex MySQL queries utilizing `JOIN`, `GROUP BY`, `ORDER BY`, and `HAVING` clauses to seamlessly merge datasets based on common IDs[cite: 3].
- Designed structured and interactive dashboards in Tableau while managing slicers and ensuring consistency between SQL outputs and visuals[cite: 3].
